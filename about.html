The author

A life with
room for wonder.

Nicholas Taylor writes fantasy and science fiction about extraordinary worlds and the people who make them matter.

Born in Denver in 1981, Nicholas lives in Colorado with his wife and family. An imaginative child, he spent plenty of time inventing stories and daydreaming about places beyond the familiar.

In his twenties, a renewed love of reading fantasy and science fiction turned into the decision to write a novel. He began Legon Awakening in the winter of 2007. It first reached readers as a weekly podcast, then in print, digital, and audio editions.

Across magical realms and distant planets, his aim remains the same: to bring readers into the scene with characters they can relate to, and give them a world worth exploring.
